Our Story

dippd is a startup founded by University of Texas at Austin (UT Austin) students Michael and Timothy. As college students balancing rigorous course loads and late-night study sessions, we found ourselves constantly searching for convenient snacks that offered both clean ingredients and high-quality taste. Our options were usually limited to highly processed candies or flavorless health bars.

The "aha!" moment occurred in our student apartment. We realized that freeze-dried fruit provides a naturally crisp, nutrient-dense base, but lacked indulgence. By combining the organic crunch of freeze-dried fruit with high-grade chocolate, we could bridge the gap between wellness and indulgence.

Timothy immediately began analyzing chocolate crystallization and moisture retention on a portable hotplate, while Michael built spreadsheets to log batches, weigh ingredients, and establish optimization curves. Through iterative prototyping, we discovered that hand-dipping exactly half of the fruit preserved cellular structure and crunch while introducing the rich, premium cocoa flavor we desired.
