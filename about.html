Our Story

Dippd started in a cramped dorm room at UT Austin. Like most college students, our late-night study sessions were fueled by convenience store snacks. But we noticed a frustrating pattern: snacks were either incredibly healthy but tasted like cardboard, or they were delicious but packed with artificial preservatives, high-fructose corn syrup, and junk.

We wanted something better. One night, while looking at a bag of freeze-dried fruit, the "aha!" moment hit us: what if we combined the light, airy crunch of freeze-dried fruit with the rich taste of artisan dark chocolate?

Timothy immediately set up a makeshift double boiler on a hotplate, while Michael pulled up a spreadsheet to track temperatures and coating thicknesses. Our first few batches were a mess—the fruit got soggy, and the chocolate was either too chalky or too thick. But we kept tweaking, measuring, and testing until we got it right.

📊 Data & Optimization

Michael

Statistics & Data Science @ UT Austin

Michael handles the distribution models. He runs data analysis to optimize batch consistency, bag weight, and flavor ratios. He ensures that every single bag of Dippd contains a mathematically balanced mix of fruit slices so you never get a bag with just one flavor.

🧪 Molecular Formulation

Timothy

Biochemistry @ UT Austin

Timothy manages the kitchen lab. He applies biochemistry principles to preserve the crystalline cell structure of our freeze-dried fruits, preventing moisture absorption. By controlling thermal temper, he makes sure the chocolate coating stays perfectly smooth and the snap stays crispy without artificial additives.

Keeping it Real

Between lectures, biochem midterms, and stats projects, we're hand-dipping every batch in Houston, TX and packing bags from our student apartment. We're building a brand the way we study: with total focus, zero shortcuts, and a slight addiction to caffeine.
